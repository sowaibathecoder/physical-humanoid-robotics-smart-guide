# Additional Resources

This section contains additional resources to support your learning in the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics course.

## Recommended Reading

### Core Textbooks
- Siciliano, B., & Khatib, O. (2016). *Springer Handbook of Robotics* (2nd ed.). Springer.
- Spong, M. W., Hutchinson, S., & Vidyasagar, M. (2006). *Robot Modeling and Control*. Wiley.

### Research Papers
- Levine, S., et al. (2023). "Offline Reinforcement Learning: From Algorithms to Practical Challenges." *Foundations and Trends in Robotics*.
- Rusu, A. A., et al. (2020). "A Generalist Agent." *arXiv preprint arXiv:2205.06175*.
